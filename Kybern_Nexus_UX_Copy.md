**WHAT WE DO**

**Section Headline**

We don't push specific technologies. We match the right tool to your stage, budget, and team.

**01 Cloud Cost Audit**
Find out exactly where your money is going — and how much you can recover. We deliver a prioritised action plan, not a 40-page PDF nobody reads.
* **What you get:**
    * Detailed breakdown of current vs. optimal spend across AWS, Azure, and GCP
    * FinOps dashboard so you can see cost trends in real time going forward
    * Resource rightsizing recommendations — what to downsize, what to reserve, what to shut off
    * Spot Instance and savings plan strategy tailored to your workloads
* **Typical outcome:** 25–40% reduction in monthly cloud spend within 60 days
* **★ NEW:** If we don't find enough recoverable waste to cover the cost of the audit, you don't pay. We're that confident.

**02 CI/CD Pipeline Rebuild**
Turn your fragile, manual deployment process into a fast, reliable, automated pipeline — with security built in, not bolted on.
* **What you get:**
    * Reusable pipeline templates your team can maintain and extend
    * Automated security scanning (SAST/DAST) integrated at every stage
    * Secret management that removes hardcoded credentials from your codebase
    * Automated rollback so a bad release doesn't become a 3am incident
* **Typical outcome:** deployment frequency 5–10x faster, with fewer incidents

**03 Kubernetes & Container Architecture**
Move from fragile, single-point-of-failure infrastructure to a resilient, auto-scaling system that handles traffic spikes without waking anyone up.
* **What you get:**
    * Full Kubernetes architecture designed around your traffic patterns and growth targets
    * Terraform state refactoring for clean, version-controlled infrastructure
    * GitOps workflows (ArgoCD / Flux) so deployments are auditable and reversible
    * Grafana observability stack so you see problems before your users do
* **Typical outcome:** 99.9%+ uptime, zero-downtime deployments

**04 Infrastructure as Code Migration**
Replace manual, undocumented click-ops with version-controlled infrastructure that's reproducible, reviewable, and auditable — just like your application code.
* **What you get:**
    * Full migration from manual provisioning to Terraform or Pulumi
    * Configuration drift detection so your infrastructure stays in sync
    * Modular IaC architecture that your team can extend without starting from scratch
    * Automated resource provisioning integrated into your CI/CD pipeline
* **Typical outcome:** provisioning time from days to minutes, zero configuration drift

### Engagement Process
**Section Headline**
`We don't show up with a predetermined solution. We diagnose before we prescribe.`
Here's what working with us looks like, from first call to final handoff.

* **Step 1 — Discovery Call (Free, 45 minutes)**
We listen first. You walk us through your current setup, your pain points, and your goals. We ask the questions your last consultant didn't. By the end, we both know whether we're a fit.
* **Step 2 — Infrastructure Audit & Diagnosis**
We go deep into your current environment. We map your architecture, identify the gaps, and quantify the cost — in dollars, in time, and in risk. You get a full findings report before we recommend anything.
* **Step 3 — Custom Architecture Design**
Based on what we found, we design a solution that fits your actual stage and budget. Not what works for a Series C company if you're Series A. The right tool for where you are now, built to grow with you.
* **Step 4 — Phased Implementation**
We build in safe, incremental phases with zero downtime. Each phase is reviewed, tested, and documented before we move to the next. No big-bang releases. No surprises.
* **Step 5 — Handoff & Team Enablement**
We leave you with complete documentation, runbooks, and a team that knows how to operate what we built. We also run a knowledge transfer session so your engineers are confident — not dependent on us.
